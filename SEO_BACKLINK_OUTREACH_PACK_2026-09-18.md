## Priority outreach sequence

### 1. Real partners

Ask a current filler, distributor, brand customer or logistics partner whether they want to cite the relevant guide in a public supplier or resource page. Request written approval before naming the relationship, project, quantity or result.

### 2. Industry editors

Offer a concise, evidence-bounded answer to one real buyer question, such as:

- how to compare a stock glass bottle with a custom mold;
- which RFQ fields prevent non-comparable packaging quotes;
- what to approve before releasing cosmetic packaging to bulk production;
- how a hotel should compare mini bottles and dispenser systems.

Link only to the directly relevant guide and disclose the company role if the editor requests a source bio.

### 3. Associations and supplier directories

Shortlist only organizations whose audience is relevant to packaging or the company's actual market. Check membership eligibility, public profile policy, editorial standards, total cost, renewal terms and whether the listing is useful without the link. Record the public URL after approval; until then the target remains unacquired.
