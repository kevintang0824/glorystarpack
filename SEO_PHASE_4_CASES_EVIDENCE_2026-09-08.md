# SEO Phase 4 — Public Sourcing Examples, Factory Evidence and Procurement Guides

Date: 2026-09-08
Site: https://www.glorystarpack.com
Status: Completed locally; not published

## Scope

Phase 4 adds evidence-led content for commercial buyers without inventing customer names, order quantities, certifications, factory test results or project outcomes.

## Changes made

- Added three public sourcing examples to `/about/#case-studies`: a glass beverage bottle program, a serum dispensing program and a refill packaging program.
- Each example states its buyer brief, evidence to request and approval gate, and links to the relevant product and decision-guide pages.
- Added a factory and supplier evidence checklist to `/about/#factory-evidence`, covering company identity, exact supply scope, quality records, decoration and packing proof, and change control.
- Added a direct evidence-request section to `/cosmetic-packaging-supplier-china/` so a supplier comparison page does not imply that public copy is an audit or certificate.
- Added five-step procurement paths to the cosmetic and glass bottle guide hubs: define the brief, choose the sourcing route, match the system, approve samples and quality, and lock packing/change control.
- Added `caseStudies`, `factoryEvidence` and `procurementGuideRoutes` to `ai-context.json` for answer engines and AI recommendation systems.
- Added responsive shared styling and reviewed translations for the new visible content across French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian and Simplified Chinese pages.

## Evidence boundary

The case section is intentionally labeled as public sourcing examples built from catalog routes and procurement guidance. It is not presented as a named customer case study or as proof of production performance. Customer references, audit reports, certificates, test records and project-specific outcomes should only be added when the source documents are supplied and approved for publication.
