About GloryStarPack

GloryStarPack, operated by Xiamen GloryStar Packaging Co., Ltd., is a Xiamen-based B2B packaging supplier. We coordinate container selection, component matching, sampling, decoration, quality review and export packing for beverage and beauty packaging projects.

What we coordinate

Packaging systems, not isolated components

Projects can include the primary bottle or jar, closure, pump, sprayer, dropper, reducer, applicator, decoration, retail box, insert and export carton. Reviewing the parts as one system helps buyers identify fit, dispensing and packing questions before bulk production.

Our current catalog covers glass bottles and jars, plastic and airless packaging, bamboo and wood components, aluminum packaging, paper-based formats, refill systems and related accessories.

Who the site is for

B2B packaging buyers

  • Private-label operators and contract fillers
  • Packaging distributors and sourcing teams
