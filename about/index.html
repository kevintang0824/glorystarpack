Company profile

A packaging supplier organized around the production brief.

GloryStarPack is the trading name of Xiamen GloryStar Packaging Co., Ltd. We coordinate glass bottles and complementary packaging for beverage, fragrance, nail care, skincare and personal-care projects.

Our role begins before a quotation is finalized. The application, capacity, filling route, bottle weight, neck finish, closure, decoration, order quantity and destination all affect the suitable production route. Reviewing those details together helps buyers avoid mismatched components and incomplete quotes.

For custom work, the project may include stock-bottle selection, new mold assessment, color and surface samples, closure matching, carton design and shipment planning. Production claims, test requirements and documents are confirmed against the exact item rather than presented as universal promises.

Production support

Four decisions shape a production-ready pack.

The exact scope varies by bottle and order. These are the checkpoints we use to structure a useful sample and quotation conversation.

Structure & mold

Bottle geometry and forming route

Review capacity, glass weight, base, shoulder, neck finish, stock availability and whether a custom mold is technically and commercially appropriate.

Surface craft

Decoration built around the material

Compare frosting, coating, screen printing, hot stamping, decals, embossing and labels against bottle shape, artwork and expected handling.

Component fit

Closures matched to the neck

Coordinate corks, caps, pumps, sprayers, droppers, collars, brushes, liners and dip tubes with the exact bottle and filling requirement.

Packing route

Protection designed before shipment

Plan dividers, cartons, inserts and pallet requirements around fragile glass, mixed components, destination and transport method.

Quality and documentation route

Approve the exact project, not a generic promise.

Testing and documentation depend on the material, formula, closure, use case and destination. The applicable checks should be written into the project brief before bulk production.

  1. Define use

    Formula or beverage, capacity, fill process, quantity and market.

  2. Match parts

    Bottle, neck, closure, liner, pump output and decoration route.

  3. Review samples

    Appearance, dimensions, fit, dispensing and production-intent details.

  4. Confirm checks

    Agree the leakage, fit, compatibility or packing checks that apply.

  5. Lock shipment

    Approve carton, divider, labeling and export-packing information.
