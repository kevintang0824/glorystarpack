If You Need a Quote

  • Prepare product type, capacity, material and closure.
  • Confirm decoration method and order quantity.
  • Share destination country, timeline and reference photos.

If You Need Samples

  • Start with stock samples for fit and capacity.
  • Approve decorated samples before bulk production.
  • Test filled samples for leakage and formula compatibility.

If You Need OEM

  • Confirm custom mold, color and finish requirements.
  • Prepare vector artwork and Pantone references.
  • Lock repeat-order specifications after sample approval.
