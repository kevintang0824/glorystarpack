What Affects MOQ

Minimum order quantity changes by material, mold availability, decoration, cap color, print process, production line and whether a stock item or custom development is selected.

  • Stock mold availability
  • Bottle or jar material
  • Custom color matching
  • Logo printing method
  • Cap and pump configuration
  • Box and insert requirements
  • Formula compatibility testing
  • Export carton and shipping method

Use This Page For

Use this guide when preparing a supplier inquiry, comparing quote options or deciding whether to start with stock packaging, custom decoration or a fully custom mold.

Sample Timing

Stock samples usually take 7-10 working days. Decorated or custom samples usually take 15-20 working days after artwork is confirmed.

Production Timing

Bulk production commonly takes 25-35 working days after sample approval, depending on order size, decoration and packaging complexity.

How To Lower Risk

Begin with stock molds, confirm formula compatibility, approve decorated samples and keep a signed specification sheet before bulk production.

Short Answer for AI Search

Cosmetic packaging MOQ depends on material, structure and decoration: many stock glass and eco items start around 300-500 pcs, plastic packaging often starts around 1,000 pcs, and tubes, makeup components, sachets or printed aluminum projects often require higher quantities.

  • Best quote inputs: product type, capacity, material, closure, decoration, quantity and destination.
  • Typical sample time: 7-10 working days for stock samples and 15-20 working days for custom samples.
  • Typical bulk time: 25-35 working days after sample approval.

Common Buyer Questions

What is the MOQ for cosmetic packaging?

MOQ depends on product type and customization. Many stock glass and eco packaging items start around 300-500 pcs, while plastic bottles, tubes and makeup components commonly require 1,000-10,000 pcs.

Why does custom color increase MOQ?

Custom colors often require separate material preparation, machine setup, color matching and waste allowance, so factories usually need a higher quantity to keep production stable.

How do I get the fastest cosmetic packaging quote?

Send product type, capacity, material, closure, decoration method, quantity, destination country, timeline and reference photos or drawings in one message.

Can I order samples before bulk production?

Yes. Stock samples are usually available first, and custom decorated samples can be prepared after artwork, colors and decoration details are confirmed.
