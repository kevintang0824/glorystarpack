Define the complete jar assembly

A cream jar is a component system, not only a glass container. Specify the body, cap, liner or inner disk, any overcap or applicator, and the required decoration as one bill of materials.

  • Glass jar body and nominal capacity
  • Cap material, thread and surface finish
  • Liner, sealing disk or inner cover
  • Spatula or other product-access accessory
  • Label area, printing, coating or frosting brief

Focus this page on glass

This page is for buyers already evaluating glass cream jars. Glass weight, wall profile, finish, closure engagement and packed-product handling should be checked on the selected model.

Capacity and use-case briefing guide

Nominal briefCommon project directionWhat to confirm
15gEye cream, balm or trial formatActual fill weight, finger or spatula access, liner fit and label area.
30gFace cream or targeted treatmentHeadspace, cap engagement, decoration registration and packed dimensions.
50gMoisturizer, mask or standard skincare formatFormula compatibility, closure torque, filled weight and transport packout.
100gBody care, mask or professional formatHandling weight, opening diameter, case count and shipping conditions.

Capacity labels are a starting brief. Available brimful capacity, nominal fill and component combinations vary by model, so approve the exact assembly rather than relying on the size name alone.

Formula and access

Review viscosity, ingredient contact, filling temperature and how the customer removes the product. Confirm comfortable access and acceptable residue with the intended formula.

Closure and decoration

Check cap engagement, target torque, liner seating and opening cycles. Approve color, artwork position and adhesion on production-intent decorated samples.

Transport checks

Evaluate leakage, cap movement, glass damage and decoration scuffing in the proposed inner pack and master carton under the project's shipping conditions.

Quick sourcing answer

Use this page when the selected packaging route is a glass cream jar. Send the supplier the target fill, formula notes, preferred capacity, cap and liner construction, decoration, order quantity, destination and launch timing.

  • Confirm glass composition and dimensions by model.
  • Test formula contact, closure fit and filling on production-intent samples.
  • Approve the complete decorated and packed assembly before production.

Glass cream jar sample checklist

  • Record brimful capacity, target fill weight and headspace.
  • Inspect glass finish, dimensions and visible defects against the approved sample.
  • Confirm cap thread engagement, target torque and repeated opening.
  • Check liner or inner-disk seating after filling and conditioning.
  • Review artwork color, position, adhesion and rub resistance.
  • Run formula compatibility, leakage and packed transport checks appropriate to the project.

Common buyer questions

What belongs in a glass cream jar sourcing brief?

Specify nominal capacity, fill weight, formula type, jar and lid finish, liner or inner disk, decoration, quantity, destination and filling method. Confirm the selected model with samples.

How should a glass cream jar sample be checked?

Check filled weight and headspace, product access, cap engagement and torque, liner seating, decoration, leakage and packed transport performance with the intended formula and production-intent components.

Where can I compare non-glass cosmetic jars?

Use the cosmetic jars category page to compare glass, PETG, acrylic, PP, aluminum and refillable jar systems before selecting a material route.
