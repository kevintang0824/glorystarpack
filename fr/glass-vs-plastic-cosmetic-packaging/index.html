Verre ou plastique pour vos cosmétiques ?

Comparez la masse, la résistance aux chutes, la barrière requise, la compatibilité et le circuit de distribution. Le verre et les plastiques présentent plusieurs compositions et constructions : comparez des configurations précises, avec leurs fermetures, plutôt que deux noms de matériaux.

Quick sourcing answer

Glass cosmetic packaging is usually best for premium skincare, fragrance, oils and brands that need a heavier shelf feel. Plastic cosmetic packaging is usually best for lightweight shipping, travel products, bath and body care, airless pumps, tubes and higher-volume programs. The final decision should consider formula compatibility, MOQ, decoration, breakage risk, freight cost and refill strategy.

Choose Glass When

  • The formula is oil, serum, fragrance or premium cream.
  • The packaging needs shelf weight and a luxury feel.
  • Frosting, coating or metallic decoration is important.

Choose Plastic When

  • The product ships through ecommerce or travel channels.
  • The format needs squeezing, airless pumping or impact resistance.
  • High-volume cost control is more important than weight.

Ask Before Quoting

  • Will the formula react with the material?
  • What decoration and cap color are required?
  • What shipping country and carton standard are needed?

Formula-Based Picks

  • Facial oil: glass dropper, amber glass or roll-on bottle.
  • Active serum: airless pump bottle or UV-protective glass.
  • Cleanser: tube, PET bottle, HDPE bottle or foam pump.
  • Body lotion: PET/HDPE bottle with pump or disc cap.
  • Fragrance: glass perfume bottle, vial or atomizer.

Common Buyer Questions

Is glass always more sustainable than plastic?

Not always. Glass can be recyclable and premium, but it is heavier to ship and needs protective packing. PCR plastic, refill pouches and refillable systems can reduce freight weight and waste in some programs.

Can I mix glass and plastic in one product line?

Yes. Many brands use glass for serum and cream, plastic for cleanser or body care, and refill pouches for repeat purchases. The key is matching color, cap style and decoration so the line still feels consistent.
