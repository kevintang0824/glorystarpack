OEM vs ODM cosmetic packaging: what is the difference?

OEM cosmetic packaging means manufacturing to the buyer's defined specification, including the selected container, closure, artwork, finish and packing method. ODM cosmetic packaging adds supplier-led selection or development when some of those decisions are still open.

This page covers the approval workflow for both routes. Buyers who first need to decide how much of the package itself must change should review custom cosmetic packaging; buyers sourcing by location can review our China cosmetic packaging supplier route.

Plan the OEM/ODM project in approval stages

A useful OEM brief separates fixed requirements from open choices. Formula or application, capacity, destination, quantity and launch timing normally guide the project first; bottle shape, closure, decoration and secondary packaging can then be compared against those constraints.

  • Route selection: decide whether a stock container with decoration is sufficient or whether a custom mold is required.
  • Component review: match the container, neck finish, pump or cap, liner, applicator and carton clearance.
  • Sample approval: freeze artwork, color, finish and production-intent component versions before bulk planning.
  • Reorder control: retain item codes, drawings, artwork revisions, approved samples and packing instructions.

Use the packaging RFQ guide to prepare inputs, then review compatibility testing and decoration methods before final approval.

Quick Answers

What is OEM cosmetic packaging?

OEM cosmetic packaging is manufacturing based on a buyer-approved specification for the container, closure, decoration, artwork and packing configuration.

What is ODM cosmetic packaging?

ODM cosmetic packaging includes supplier-led component selection or development before samples and the final production specification are approved.

Can I request samples for an OEM or ODM project?

Yes. Sample availability, scope, charges and timing are confirmed after the selected components, decoration, approval goal and destination are defined. A visual stock sample does not replace a production-intent component set for fit, filling, dispensing or decoration review.

What information is needed for a quote?

Share the application or formula, capacity, component system, decoration and artwork status, quantity per SKU, destination, timing, sample goal and reference photos or drawings. Final MOQ, availability and timing depend on the selected configuration.
