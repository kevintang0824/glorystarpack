Accessory Scope

Use this collection when the bottle shape is not enough and your project needs the closure, dispensing, sealing and sample approval details solved together.

  • Lotion pumps, treatment pumps and foam pump heads
  • Fine mist sprayers and mini trigger sprayers
  • Disc top caps, flip top caps, screw caps and collars
  • Glass pipettes, droppers and rollerball inserts
  • Orifice reducers, induction liners, gaskets and shrink bands
  • Spatulas, scoops, applicators and sample tools
  • Color matching boards and buyer presentation trays

Best For

Skincare bottles, cream jars, cosmetic tubes, serum droppers, perfume bottles, room sprays, refill pouches, travel sets, hotel amenities and private label launch kits that require matched components instead of isolated products.

Why It Matters

Small parts decide leakage, dispensing feel, shelf presentation and reorder stability. A one-stop accessory kit helps buyers confirm fit and finish before mass production.

FAQ

Can accessories be sampled before mass production?

Yes. Accessory starter kits and sample trays can be prepared so buyers can compare fit, finish, output and decoration direction.

Can pumps and caps match a custom bottle color?

Yes. Standard colors, custom colors, metallic collars, matte finishes and sample boards can be coordinated with the bottle or jar family.

Do liners and reducers need formula testing?

Yes. Liner material, reducer hole size, cap fit and seal strength should be checked with the formula, filling method and shipping conditions.
