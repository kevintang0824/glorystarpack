FAQ

Can beard oil bottles and balm tins be ordered together?

Yes. Beard oil droppers, roll-ons, balm tins, pomade jars, sleeves, cartons and molded inserts can be coordinated as one project.

Can packaging be customized for a barbershop or men's care brand?

Yes. Bottle color, cap finish, tin coating, jar color, label area, logo printing, carton and gift box structure can be customized.

Can you supply travel grooming kits?

Yes. Mini pumps, foamers, jars, tins, roll-ons, pouches, insert cards and small boxes can be packed as travel or discovery kits.
