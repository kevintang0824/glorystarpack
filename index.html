Buyer FAQ

Glass bottle answers before you send an inquiry.

For a more precise recommendation, share the application, target capacity, quantity, closure, decoration and destination country.

Explore beverage bottle specifications →

What types of glass bottles do you supply?

We supply glass bottles for liquor and spirits, wine, beer, perfume, nail polish, skincare and specialty beverage projects, together with matched closures and selected secondary packaging.

Can you develop custom glass bottle molds and decoration?

Yes. A project can include stock-bottle selection or custom mold development, plus frosting, coating, screen printing, hot stamping, decals, embossing and closure matching where applicable.

What is the minimum order quantity for glass bottles?

MOQ depends on the bottle model, stock status, glass color, closure and decoration. Share the required capacity, quantity and finish so the applicable starting quantity can be confirmed.

What information is needed for a glass bottle quote?

A useful brief includes the application, capacity, target fill, closure, quantity, decoration, destination country and any pressure, filling-temperature or line-compatibility requirements.

How are beer and sparkling beverage bottle requirements reviewed?

Carbonation pressure, filling temperature, pasteurization route, neck finish, closure and packing method must be reviewed against the exact bottle specification and production-intent samples.

Can you arrange worldwide delivery?

We can discuss express, air or sea shipment options and plan dividers, cartons and export packing around the selected glass bottle and destination.
