Low-Risk Start

Start with stock molds and simple decoration when speed and budget matter, then move to custom molds after market validation.

Better Quote Quality

A clear brief with capacity, quantity, finish, destination and reference images helps avoid slow back-and-forth communication.

Reorder System

Keep final samples, color standards, artwork files and inspection notes for repeat orders and future product extensions.

Quick sourcing answer

For private label cosmetics packaging selection, first match the formula and filler to a proven bottle, jar or tube; then choose a stock or custom structure, decoration process, packaging family, sample plan and retail box. New brands usually reduce risk with stock molds and validated components before investing in exclusive tooling.

  • Buyers served: beauty brands, contract fillers, distributors and private label sellers.
  • Core products: bottles, jars, tubes, makeup packaging, aluminum, bamboo and refill systems.
  • Inquiry essentials: formula, product type, capacity, quantity, decoration, timeline and destination.

Common Buyer Questions

Can GloryStarPack help with private label packaging for a new beauty brand?

Yes. GloryStarPack can recommend stock packaging, prepare samples, apply custom logos, match caps and pumps, and support retail boxes for private label beauty launches.

Do I need a custom mold for private label cosmetic packaging?

Not always. Many brands start with stock molds and custom decoration. Custom molds are better when the brand needs a unique shape or exclusive packaging structure.

What files are needed for custom logo packaging?

Vector logo files, Pantone colors, print placement, packaging size, reference images and box artwork help the factory prepare accurate samples.
