Common Buyer Questions

What formulas are best for airless pump bottles?

Airless pump bottles are often used for serum, eye cream, active skincare, lotion, sunscreen, primer and foundation because they support controlled dispensing and reduced air exposure.

Can airless bottles be refillable?

Yes. Refillable airless systems can use a reusable outer shell and replaceable inner cartridge depending on the selected structure.

What should be tested before ordering airless packaging?

Test formula viscosity, pump output, leakage, actuator feel, filling compatibility, storage stability and decoration durability before bulk production.
