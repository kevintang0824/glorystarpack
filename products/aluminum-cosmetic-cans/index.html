Common Buyer Questions

Are aluminum cosmetic cans suitable for aerosols?

Yes, aluminum aerosol cans can be used for dry shampoo, body mist and deodorant spray when the valve, actuator and filling requirements are matched by project.

Can you make beer-can style beauty packaging?

Yes. Beer-can and soda-can style aluminum containers are available for novelty beauty gifts, bath powder, body care and promotional sets.

What should I confirm before ordering aluminum cans?

Confirm product format, filling method, pressure requirements, cap or valve style, decoration, order quantity and destination market.
