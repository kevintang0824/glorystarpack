Buyer questions

Beverage bottle sourcing FAQ.

Packaging performance depends on the exact bottle, closure and filling process, so physical compatibility checks remain part of the approval path.

What beverage bottle styles can you supply?

The range includes Bordeaux and Burgundy wine bottles, sparkling bottles, heavy-base whiskey bottles, vodka and gin bottles, mini liquor bottles and swing-top specialty beverage bottles.

Can bottles and closures be matched together?

Yes. Bottle neck finish, cork or cap, liner, tamper component, decoration, label area and export carton can be reviewed as one project.

What is needed for a quote?

Share the beverage type, capacity, bottle style, glass color, closure, decoration, quantity, filling conditions, destination country and reference image.

Are samples available?

Availability depends on the model. Physical bottle and closure samples are recommended for capacity, fit, decoration and filling-line checks.
