Formula and filling

Share viscosity, ingredient-contact notes, target fill, filling temperature and line method. Test the shortlisted assemblies with the intended bulk formula.

Usability and closure

Review opening diameter, finger or spatula access, cap engagement, liner seating, opening cycles and any refill-removal step.

Decoration and packout

Approve color and artwork on production-intent components, then assess leakage, scuffing and component movement in the proposed transport carton.

Common buyer questions

Which cosmetic jar material route should I shortlist?

Start with formula contact, filling method, product access, target pack weight, decoration and shipping conditions. Compare complete assemblies in the shortlisted materials with the intended formula.

Which components belong in a cosmetic jar specification?

Specify the jar body, cap, liner or sealing disk, inner cup or refill pod when used, applicator, decoration and transport packout as one system.

When should I use the glass cream jars page?

Use the glass cream jars page after choosing glass for a face cream, eye cream, balm or mask project and when capacity, closure, filling access and transport checks are the main sourcing questions.
