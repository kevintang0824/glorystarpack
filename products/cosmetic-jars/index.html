Common Buyer Questions

Which cosmetic jar material should I choose?

Glass is best for premium feel, PETG and acrylic are lighter luxury options, PP is practical for masks and scrubs, and bamboo or aluminum can support eco-positioning.

Can cosmetic jars be customized with a logo?

Yes. Decoration can include silk screen printing, hot stamping, labels, color coating, frosting, metallized caps and custom retail boxes.

What should I send for a cosmetic jar quote?

Share capacity, jar material, lid style, decoration method, order quantity, formula notes, destination country and reference photos.
