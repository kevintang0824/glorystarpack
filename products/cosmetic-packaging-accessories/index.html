FAQ

Can accessories be sampled before mass production?

Yes. Accessory starter kits and sample trays can be prepared so buyers can compare fit, finish, output and decoration direction.

Can pumps and caps match a custom bottle color?

Yes. Standard colors, custom colors, metallic collars, matte finishes and sample boards can be coordinated with the bottle or jar family.

Do liners and reducers need formula testing?

Yes. Liner material, reducer hole size, cap fit and seal strength should be checked with the formula, filling method and shipping conditions.
