Common Buyer Questions

Can I request a complete packaging kit instead of separate products?

Yes. Share your formula type, capacity, target market, decoration style, quantity and timeline, and GloryStarPack can propose a matched kit.

Can boxes and inserts be included?

Yes. Folding cartons, rigid boxes, molded pulp inserts, paper inserts and export dividers can be developed with the packaging set.

Can the kit include custom decoration?

Yes. Logo printing, hot stamping, labels, coating, frosting, color matching and sample proofing can be included in one project.
