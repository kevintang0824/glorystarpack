Common Buyer Questions

How do I choose the right pump or closure?

Match the closure to formula viscosity, bottle neck finish, output dosage, leakage requirements and user experience.

Can pump colors and finishes be customized?

Yes. Pumps and caps can be supplied in standard colors, custom colors, metallic finishes and matching collar styles depending on MOQ.

What details are needed for closure matching?

Share bottle neck finish, bottle height, formula type, expected dosage, cap style, color, quantity and reference samples.

Can you supply a full kit instead of separate parts?

Yes. GloryStarPack can bundle bottles, jars, tubes, pumps, caps, droppers, applicators, cartons, inserts and export packing so the buyer saves sourcing time.
