FAQ

Can I combine vials, sachets and jars in one kit?

Yes. Sample discovery kits can combine perfume vials, droppers, sample sachets, mini jars, trays and cartons in one project.

Can the packaging be customized?

Yes. Labels, logo printing, hot stamping, custom sleeves, molded inserts, retail boxes and export cartons can be developed together.

What information is needed for a quote?

Please share sample type, filling volume, formula, quantity, decoration, packing style, destination country and any reference photos.
