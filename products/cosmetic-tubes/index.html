Specify the tube structure, opening and filling route together

Tube selection is more than choosing a nominal capacity. The body structure, shoulder, nozzle, cap, decoration area and sealing method must suit the formula and the filler’s equipment. A production-intent sample should represent the intended material construction and closure rather than only the printed appearance.

  • Share formula type, viscosity, fill volume and any barrier or light-protection concern.
  • Confirm tube diameter and length, wall or laminate structure, nozzle opening, cap style and tamper-evidence requirement.
  • Review artwork distortion, seam or eye-mark requirements, fill-end seal area and carton orientation.
  • Repeat compatibility and dispensing checks if the body material, adhesive, ink, cap or formula changes.

Use the compatibility-testing guide and decoration-method guide to prepare the sample approval plan.
