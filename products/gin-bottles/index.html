Bottle identity

Make the silhouette do useful brand work.

A highly unusual shape is not automatically better. The strongest gin bottle balances recognizability with a practical label panel, stable base, compatible closure, filling-line handling and export carton dimensions.

Nordic & Round

Clean, balanced forms for classic, modern and private-label gin programs.

  • Good label application area
  • Clear or color-coated directions
  • Bar-top and screw models

Fluted & Apothecary

Textured or heritage-inspired profiles that support botanical positioning.

  • Review decoration on curved surfaces
  • Embossing by project feasibility
  • Gift-box insert coordination

Mini & Discovery

Small gin bottles for sampling, hotel service, tasting kits and event gifting.

  • 50ml to 200ml project formats
  • Tamper closure matching
  • Tray and carton planning

Buyer questions

Custom gin bottle FAQ.

Approve bottle form, decoration and closure together to avoid late-stage fit or artwork changes.

Which shapes suit gin packaging?

Common directions include Nordic round, short-shoulder, apothecary, fluted, rectangular and mini bottle styles.

Can gin bottles use custom colors?

Depending on the project, options can include clear glass, frosting, translucent or solid coating, and colored-glass models.

Can botanical details be added?

Botanical themes can be expressed through labels, screen printing, color coating or project-specific embossing review.

What is needed for a quote?

Share capacity, silhouette, color, closure, decoration, quantity, destination and a reference image or drawing.
