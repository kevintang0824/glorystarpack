Closure fitConfirm thread, torque, liner, reducer, pump output and pipette length.
Decoration areaReview screen print limits, hot stamping area, label panel and curved surfaces.
Export packingUse dividers, trays, cartons and drop-test packing for fragile glass shipments.

Glass Packaging FAQ

Is glass better than plastic for cosmetic packaging?

Glass is best for premium shelf presence, fragrance, facial oils and formulas that need strong compatibility. Plastic is better when low weight, squeeze feel, impact resistance or high-volume cost matters.

Can I mix glass bottles, caps and boxes in one order?

Yes. GloryStarPack can coordinate bottles, droppers, pumps, caps, labels, retail boxes, inserts and export packing as one packaging project.

What files are useful for custom glass packaging?

Reference photos, capacity, dimensions, artwork, Pantone color, logo file, decoration method, target order quantity and destination country are the most useful details.
