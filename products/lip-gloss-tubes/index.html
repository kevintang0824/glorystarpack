Common Buyer Questions

What lip gloss tube materials are available?

Common options include PETG, AS, ABS, PP wipers and aluminum or metallized caps. Clear PETG is popular because it shows formula color clearly.

Can you match the applicator to my formula?

Yes. Applicator shape, flocking, wiper opening and cap weight can be reviewed based on formula viscosity and desired pickup.

What should I send for a lip gloss tube quote?

Share capacity, tube shape, cap material, applicator type, decoration method, order quantity, destination country and reference photos.
