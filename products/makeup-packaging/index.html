Approve makeup packaging as a component system

Color-cosmetic packaging performance depends on the container, wiper, stem, applicator, closure and formula working together. Capacity alone cannot predict pickup, payoff, leakage or the amount of product retained around the neck, so samples should represent the intended production components and decoration.

  • For liquid lip products, confirm bottle profile, neck, wiper restriction, stem length, applicator shape and closure torque.
  • For compacts and sticks, review pan or mechanism fit, fill process, closure security, decoration area and drop protection.
  • Check artwork against curved surfaces, parting lines and the visible orientation after the cap is tightened.
  • Record approved component codes and sample versions so a reorder does not silently substitute a functional part.

For a focused component checklist, use the custom lip gloss tubes guide. Buyers building a wider line can also review the color cosmetics component systems guide and compare cosmetic tubes using the same approval logic.
