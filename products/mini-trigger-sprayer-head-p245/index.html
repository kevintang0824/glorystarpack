Product planning summary

Configuration and intended use

Trigger sprayers can be matched by neck finish, output, lock type, dip tube length and bottle shape. Useful for salon, body care and refill programs.

Checks before sampling

  • Match the component to the exact bottle neck finish or approved finish drawing, not diameter alone.
  • Confirm formula viscosity, target output, actuator or spray pattern, dip-tube length and filling method.
  • Validate priming, leakage, lock or overcap function and transport handling with the final bottle system.

Application and sizing guide

Mini trigger sprayer sizing

A mini trigger sprayer gives a compact grip for body care, hair care, sanitizer and selected personal-care refill bottles. Review the 24/410, 28/410 or custom neck with the bottle shoulder, label area and hand clearance, then validate spray pattern, priming, dip-tube angle and leakage on production-intent samples.
