Application and sizing guide

Mini trigger sprayer sizing

A mini trigger sprayer gives a compact grip for body care, hair care, sanitizer and selected personal-care refill bottles. Review the 24/410, 28/410 or custom neck with the bottle shoulder, label area and hand clearance, then validate spray pattern, priming, dip-tube angle and leakage on production-intent samples.
