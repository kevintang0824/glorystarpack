Plastic Packaging Engineering Checks

Plastic packaging decisions affect filling, leakage, squeeze feel, freight cost and consumer use. Confirm these items before approving samples.

If the project uses pressure-sensitive labels, use the cosmetic bottle label adhesion guide to define the actual plastic surface, label curve, adhesive, application conditions and water or formula-spill tests.

Material selectionPET for clarity, HDPE for impact and squeeze, PP for mono-material systems, PCR for lower virgin plastic.
Closure fitCheck neck finish, torque, hinge strength, dip tube length, actuator comfort and overcap clearance.
Formula behaviorReview viscosity, alcohol, oils, SPF, pigment, fragrance and expected storage conditions.
Decoration riskConfirm print adhesion, label curve, soft-touch coating, shrink sleeve or hot stamping before bulk.

Buyer questions

Plastic Packaging FAQ

Which plastic material should I choose for cosmetic packaging?

PET and PETG are clear and good for toner or lotion bottles, HDPE is durable and practical for shampoo or body care, PP is common for caps, jars and mono-material airless systems, and PCR options can support lower virgin plastic goals.

Can pumps, caps and bottles be tested together?

Yes. Closure fit, torque, pump output, dip tube length, leakage and carton packing should be tested with the actual bottle and formula before bulk production.

Can you supply plastic packaging and glass packaging together?

Yes. GloryStarPack can coordinate glass bottles, plastic bottles, tubes, pumps, caps, boxes, inserts and export packing for one launch family.
