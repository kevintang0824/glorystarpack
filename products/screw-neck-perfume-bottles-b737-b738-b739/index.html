Compare three capacities within one bottle family

A fragrance range can use a smaller bottle for a compact format and larger bottles for the main collection. Compare the actual bottle dimensions, weight, decoration area and finished assembly instead of assuming that the three capacities require identical labels, pumps or cartons. The 30ml, 50ml and 100ml models are quoted individually; an order split across capacities must meet the 1,000-piece minimum for each model.

Match the bottle, sprayer and cap

For these screw-neck bottles, request the current thread and sealing-surface drawing for the selected model. Confirm the screw-on sprayer, gasket, collar, dip tube and cap against that drawing. A shared 15mm nominal neck does not establish that the same pump or cap fits all three bottles. Test the filled pack for spray performance, leakage, cap seating and handling before approval.

Specify the required cap shape, surface finish and color when requesting samples. Approve the bottle and dispensing components together with the intended fragrance.

MOQ, samples and production timing

The minimum order is 1,000 bottles per model. Include your chosen pump, cap and decoration in the inquiry so the quotation identifies exactly what is included. Bottle availability, sample arrangements, decoration options, lead time and shipping are confirmed for the selected configuration and destination.

For a complete quote, send the model, capacity, quantity per model, desired decoration, pump and cap requirements, destination country and target delivery date. If the component set is undecided, ask for a matching sample proposal before approving the final specification.
