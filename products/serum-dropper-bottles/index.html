Common Buyer Questions

What size serum dropper bottle should I choose?

15ml and 30ml are common for facial serum, while 5ml and 10ml are useful for samples, oils and travel sets. 50ml and 60ml are used for larger treatment products.

Can you supply amber serum dropper bottles?

Yes. Amber and other UV-protective glass options are available for formulas that need better light protection.

What information is needed for a dropper bottle quote?

Share capacity, glass color, dropper style, bulb color, collar finish, decoration method, quantity, destination country and reference photos.
