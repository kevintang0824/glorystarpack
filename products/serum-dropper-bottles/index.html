How should a buyer choose a serum dropper bottle?

Choose a serum dropper bottle as a complete bottle-and-closure system, not by bottle color alone. Define the formula, viscosity, fill volume, target dose, neck finish, gasket, bulb, collar and pipette. Then test production-intent components with the actual formula for draw, dose, drip control, leakage, torque, formula contact, decoration and packed distribution. Treat light-management and child-resistant requirements as evidence-led project specifications.

Serum Dropper Bottle FAQ

How should a buyer choose a serum dropper bottle?

Define the formula, viscosity, fill volume, target dose and use pattern before choosing the bottle. Match the neck finish, gasket, bulb, collar and pipette as one system, then test production-intent components with the actual formula for draw, dose, drip control, leakage, torque, formula contact, decoration and packed distribution.

Does amber glass prove light protection for a serum?

No. Amber glass, a coating or an opaque secondary pack may form part of a light-management strategy, but the required wavelength range, transmission target, formula and shelf-life protocol are project-specific. Approve a protection claim only from evidence for the exact production-intent package and formula.

What should be tested in a dropper assembly?

Test the exact bottle, neck, gasket, collar, bulb and pipette with the actual formula and intended fill. Review component fit, torque, sealing, draw and release, dose consistency, dripping, leakage in relevant orientations, formula contact, decoration and the packed distribution route using agreed acceptance criteria.
