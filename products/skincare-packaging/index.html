Select the skincare format around formula access and dosage

Skincare bottles, jars, droppers, pumps and tubes expose the formula in different ways. Define viscosity, fill volume, target dose, application area and expected use period first, then compare the product-contact construction and closure system with the filler’s equipment.

  • Use pump or airless formats when controlled dispensing and reduced direct finger contact are important to the brief.
  • Use jars when the intended texture, access and application method suit a wide opening, after liner and closure fit are reviewed.
  • Confirm dip-tube length, pump output, dropper fit, gasket or liner, neck finish and carton clearance with production-intent parts.
  • Repeat compatibility and dispensing checks when the formula or any contact component changes.

Buyers comparing wide-mouth formats can continue to the cosmetic jars sourcing guide. For controlled liquid delivery, compare airless bottles and serum dropper bottles against the same formula and dosage brief.
