Safety-critical selection

The bottle specification comes before the decoration.

Sparkling products can place internal pressure on the package. Bottle geometry, glass distribution, neck finish, closure and process conditions must therefore be reviewed as a technical system. A still-wine bottle should not be assumed suitable.

Buyer questions

Sparkling wine bottle FAQ.

Final approval must rely on the selected bottle specification, production process and closure system.

Can a standard wine bottle be used?

Do not select a sparkling wine bottle by appearance alone. The bottle must be approved for the intended internal pressure and process.

Which sizes can be sourced?

Common project directions include 200ml, 375ml and 750ml bottles, plus larger formats. Availability depends on the selected bottle.

Which closures are used?

Projects may use a sparkling cork with wire hood or a crown closure during a production stage, depending on the process and neck finish.

What is needed for a quote?

Share beverage type, capacity, target pressure, production method, bottle color, closure, quantity, destination and filling conditions.
