Buyer questions

Wholesale vodka bottle FAQ.

Final glass clarity, dimensions and closure compatibility depend on the chosen bottle and approved technical specification.

What sizes are commonly requested?

Common project sizes include 50ml and 100ml minis, 200ml and 375ml formats, and 500ml, 700ml, 750ml and 1L retail bottles.

Why use extra-flint glass?

Extra-flint glass is selected when a brand wants high clarity and a cleaner colorless presentation. Final clarity depends on the selected model.

Can clear bottles be frosted?

Yes. Depending on the project, frosting, translucent coating, solid color, screen print, labels and metallic decoration can be reviewed.

What is needed for a quote?

Share capacity, bottle height or shape, glass clarity, closure, finish, decoration, quantity, destination and line constraints.
