Wine bottle styles

Shape carries both function and shelf identity.

Bottle style influences label proportions, carton dimensions, filling-line clearance and closure choice. Final specifications depend on the selected model, so drawings and physical samples are part of approval.

Bordeaux Bottle

A straight-sided profile with defined shoulders and a broad label panel.

  • Common for red, rosé and table wine
  • 375ml, 750ml and 1.5L formats
  • Cork or ROPP models

Burgundy Bottle

A softer, sloping shoulder shape for wine and specialty beverage presentations.

  • Olive, antique green and flint options
  • Label and direct print review
  • Cork finish by bottle model

Hock & Dessert Wine

Tall, slender shapes for Riesling, ice wine, fruit wine and premium smaller-volume presentations.

  • 200ml to 750ml starting formats
  • Antique green, flint or colored glass
  • Cork or BVS screw finish by model

Sparkling & Mini

Heavy sparkling bottles and smaller portion formats for tastings and gift programs.

  • 200ml to 750ml sparkling formats
  • 187ml to 375ml mini formats
  • Pressure or closure checks by use case

Procurement answers

Wine bottle FAQ.

Every bottle and closure combination should be approved using the correct technical drawing and physical sample.

What wine bottle shapes are available?

Sourcing includes Bordeaux, Burgundy, Hock, Riesling, dessert, sparkling and mini formats for tasting or gift sets.

Which glass colors can be sourced?

Common options include flint, amber, olive green, antique green and champagne green, depending on the selected model.

Can corks and caps be matched?

Yes. Cork, ROPP screw cap, crown or sparkling closure options are matched to the exact neck finish and verified by drawing and sample.

What should I send for a quote?

Send wine type, capacity, shape, color, closure, decoration, quantity, destination and any filling-line or pressure requirements.
