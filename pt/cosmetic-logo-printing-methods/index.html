Quick sourcing answer

Choose the quote route from the artwork and component: silk screen for limited-color logos, hot stamp for a foil effect, UV or digital printing for detailed multicolor artwork, and labels when copy or artwork may change. Feasibility and price depend on the exact substrate, surface curve, print area, color count, quantity and approved proof.

  • Record component and artwork item codes.

Common Quote Questions

Which route fits a small or changing artwork run?

A label or a limited-area print may be worth quoting first, but setup, stock, application and quantity still depend on the exact component. Send both the artwork and the intended pack instead of choosing from method names alone.

Can one brand color look identical on every material?

Glass, plastic, metal, paper, ink and foil create different visual results. Set a target and approve a physical proof for each substrate rather than treating one digital color value as the finished standard.
