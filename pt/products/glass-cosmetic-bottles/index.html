Frascos cosméticos de vidro

Escolha o perfil, a cor e a capacidade útil em conjunto com o gargalo e o fecho. Confirme dimensões, peso, área de rotulagem e condições de enchimento. A espessura do vidro não comprova, por si só, resistência ao impacto ou à pressão.

How should a buyer choose a glass cosmetic bottle?

Choose a glass cosmetic bottle from the formula, fill process and dispensing task. Define fill volume and temperature, glass color, neck finish, liner or gasket, pump, dropper, sprayer or reducer, target dose, decoration and packed route. Then test the exact bottle-and-closure configuration for formula contact, sealing, dispensing, light exposure, decoration and transport. Appearance or glass color alone does not establish compatibility or protection.

Glass Cosmetic Bottle FAQ

How should a buyer choose a glass cosmetic bottle?

Start with the formula, fill process and dispensing task. Compare the exact bottle, neck, liner or gasket, pump, dropper, sprayer, reducer or brush system, then use production-intent components to verify formula contact, sealing, dose, leakage, decoration and packed distribution. A bottle category or visual sample alone is not a compatibility or performance approval.

Can a glass bottle and closure be reviewed together?

Yes. Define the glass bottle, neck finish, liner or gasket, dispenser, dip tube or pipette, cap, decoration and secondary pack as one configuration. Current fit, availability, sample scope, MOQ and timing must still be confirmed for the selected components and project.

Does amber or coated glass prove formula protection?

No. Glass color, coating or an opaque secondary pack may be part of a light-management strategy, but the required wavelength range, transmission target, formula, closure and shelf-life protocol are project-specific. Approve claims only from evidence for the exact production-intent package and formula.
