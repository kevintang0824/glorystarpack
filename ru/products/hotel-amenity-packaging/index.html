Hotel Amenity Scope

Use this category when your hospitality program needs mini packaging that can be branded, filled, packed and shipped as a consistent amenity set.

  • Mini shampoo, conditioner, shower gel and lotion bottles
  • Travel-size pump, flip-cap and screw-cap bottles
  • Small cream jars, sample jars and sachets
  • Clear toiletry pouches and paper sleeves
  • Refill pouches for lower-waste hospitality programs
  • Cartons, molded trays and export packing
  • Custom labels, logo printing and matched cap colors

Best For

Hotels, resorts, spas, airlines, travel retail buyers, amenity distributors, private label body care brands and contract fillers that need matched mini bottles, pouches, cartons and export packing.

Project Advantage

We can coordinate the full amenity set so bottle capacity, cap type, label area, pouch size, carton count and reorder specifications stay consistent.

FAQ

Can mini hotel amenity bottles be customized?

Yes. Bottle color, cap type, label area, logo printing, custom labels, paper sleeves and cartons can be customized by project.

Can pouches and mini bottles be supplied together?

Yes. Clear toiletry pouches, mini bottles, jars, sachets, sleeves and cartons can be managed as one amenity kit.

Can you support lower-waste amenity programs?

Yes. Refill pouches, reusable bottles, PCR plastic options and larger refill formats can be reviewed for hospitality refill programs.
