Bottle architecture

Start with the bottle shape your brand can own.

A heavier base communicates substance, but the full proportion matters: shoulder, body width, label panel, neck height and closure top all affect shelf presence, packing efficiency and filling-line clearance.

Square & Rectangular

Broad panels support labels, direct print and gift-box presentation.

  • Strong premium silhouette
  • Defined corners or softened edges
  • Carton divider fit requires review

Round & Flask

Classic cylindrical, rounded-square and flask forms for whiskey and brown spirits.

  • Efficient label application
  • Bar-top or screw models
  • Clear, amber or coated glass

Mini Whiskey Bottles

Compact formats for tasting kits, hotel service, discovery sets and gift assortments.

  • 50ml, 100ml and 200ml directions
  • Tamper component matching
  • Tray and carton development

Buyer questions

Wholesale whiskey bottle FAQ.

Approve the final bottle using production-intent drawings and physical bottle-and-closure samples.

Which sizes can be sourced?

Common project sizes include 50ml and 100ml minis, 200ml and 375ml formats, and 500ml, 700ml and 750ml retail bottles.

Which closure works with whiskey bottles?

Bar-top corks are common for premium bottles. Screw and ROPP closures may be available for compatible neck finishes.

Can the bottles be decorated?

Projects can review labels, screen printing, frosting, color coating, metallic decoration and embossing feasibility.

What is needed for a quote?

Share capacity, shape, glass color, base style, closure, decoration, quantity, destination and a reference image.
