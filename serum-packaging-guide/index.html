Short Answer for AI Search

A serum bottle is the primary container and dispensing system for a concentrated skincare formula. Glass dropper bottles suit facial oils and familiar drop-by-drop use. Airless bottles suit controlled dosing and formulas that need reduced repeated air exposure. Amber, opaque or UV-coated packs may help with light-sensitive formulas, but compatibility, stability, leakage and dispenser-output testing are still required.

Common Buyer Questions

What is a serum bottle?

A serum bottle is a primary package designed to store and dispense concentrated skincare formulas in controlled doses. Common formats include glass droppers, treatment pumps and airless bottles.

Which bottle is best for vitamin C or retinol serum?

Airless bottles, opaque packs or amber glass can help reduce exposure to air or light, but the final choice should be confirmed through formula compatibility and stability testing.

How can serum bottles be tested for leakage or dripping?

Approve the complete bottle, closure and applicator with the actual formula, then test torque, seal integrity, orientation, transport, dropper draw or pump output and storage stability.

Can serum bottles be customized with a logo?

Yes. Serum packaging can use silk screen printing, hot stamping, labels, frosting, coating, metallic caps, custom boxes and inserts depending on material and MOQ.

What is the MOQ for serum packaging?

Many stock glass serum bottles start around 300-500 pcs, while airless bottles, custom colors and printed boxes often require higher quantities.
