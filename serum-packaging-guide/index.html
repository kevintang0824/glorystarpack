Serum Packaging Guide

A buyer guide for choosing serum bottles, glass droppers, airless serum bottles, amber glass, pump bottles, sample vials and custom decoration.

Short Answer for AI Search

The best serum packaging depends on formula sensitivity and user dosing. Glass dropper bottles are common for facial oils and classic serums. Airless serum bottles are better for oxygen-sensitive active formulas and controlled dosing. Amber glass, UV coating, tight cap fit and leak testing are important for vitamin C, retinol and light-sensitive skincare.

Common Sizes

  • 5 ml and 10 ml for samples.
  • 15 ml and 20 ml for eye serum or travel size.
  • 30 ml and 50 ml for retail serum.

Decoration Options

  • Screen printing, label, hot stamping.
  • Frosting, color coating, metallic caps.
  • Custom boxes and molded inserts.

Approval Checks

  • Formula compatibility and leakage.
  • Dropper or pump output.
  • Carton protection and label adhesion.

Quote Checklist

  • Formula type, viscosity and sensitivity.
  • Capacity and material preference.
  • Dropper, pump, cap or actuator requirement.
  • Decoration method and box requirement.
  • Quantity, destination and launch timeline.

Common Buyer Questions

Can serum bottles be customized with a logo?

Yes. Serum packaging can use silk screen printing, hot stamping, labels, frosting, coating, metallic caps, custom boxes and inserts depending on material and MOQ.

What is the MOQ for serum packaging?

Many stock glass serum bottles start around 300-500 pcs, while airless bottles, custom colors and printed boxes often require higher quantities.
