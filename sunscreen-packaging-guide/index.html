Sunscreen tube packaging commonly uses PE, PCR PE or laminated ABL/PBL structures with flip-top or screw caps. Common Buyer Questions

What material is used for sunscreen tube packaging?

Common options include mono-layer or multilayer PE tubes, PCR PE tubes and laminated ABL or PBL tubes. The formula, barrier target, filling process and regional requirements should be checked before selection.

What sizes are common for sunscreen tubes?

Face SPF often uses 30-50 ml tubes, while body sunscreen commonly uses 50-150 ml formats. Mini 5-15 ml tubes can support samples and travel sets, subject to the filling line and closure.

How should sunscreen tubes be tested before production?

Test formula compatibility, leakage, cap seal, tube crimp strength, paneling or swelling, print adhesion and transport performance under the intended storage conditions.

Are tubes or bottles better for sunscreen?

Tubes are usually better for portable squeeze use and facial SPF. Bottles are practical for body sunscreen, family sizes and pump dispensing. The formula viscosity and filling method should decide the format.

Can sunscreen packaging use recycled content?

PCR tubes and bottles may support recycled-content goals, but PCR percentage, formula compatibility, color variation and permitted environmental claims should be confirmed for the destination market.
